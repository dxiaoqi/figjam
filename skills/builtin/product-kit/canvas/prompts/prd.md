Write a PRD outline from the board.

1. `query_blocks` for `product-kit.solution` with status `committed` (or the solution the person picked), then
   follow its connectors: the opportunity it solves, its experiments, metrics and requirements.
2. Outline:
   - **Problem** — the opportunity: who, the pain, the evidence (quote the `evidence` field; cite competitor gaps).
   - **Goal and metrics** — north star, input and guardrail metrics with baseline → target.
   - **Solution** — the committed solution in two sentences; alternatives considered (the other solutions under the
     same opportunity, with why not: impact / effort / confidence).
   - **Requirements** — grouped must / should / could / won't, by area.
   - **Validation** — experiments run and their results; what's still an assumption.
   - **Competitive context** — the rows of the competitor matrix that matter for this solution.
   - **Open questions.**
3. Use only what is on the board; mark anything you had to assume as *assumption*.
