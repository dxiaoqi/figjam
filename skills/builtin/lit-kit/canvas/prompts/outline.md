Write the literature review outline from the board.

1. `export_board { format: "md" }` and `query_blocks` for `lit-kit.question`, `lit-kit.claim`, `lit-kit.gap`.
2. Outline:
   - **Scope** — the research questions, how many papers, years covered, methods mix.
   - **One section per research question** — its claims ordered by consensus (strong first). Under each claim:
     the supporting evidence as "finding (cite, loc)", then counter-evidence, then a one-line assessment.
   - **Where the field disagrees** — contested claims side by side.
   - **Gaps and future work** — high priority first.
   - **References** — every paper card: authors, year, title, venue, link.
3. Cite only what is on the board; mark your own synthesis as such.
