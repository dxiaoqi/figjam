Write the research report from the board.

1. `export_board { format: "md" }` and `query_blocks` for `research-kit.insight`, `research-kit.opportunity` and
   `hypothesis`.
2. Structure:
   - **Summary** — three sentences: who we talked to (count and segments from the interview cards), the two most
     important findings, what we recommend next.
   - **Findings** — one section per insight, ordered by confidence then evidence: the finding, why it matters, and
     2–3 supporting quotes in their own words with `participant` and `ref`.
   - **Opportunities** — high impact first, with effort.
   - **What we'll test next** — the hypotheses, each with how we'd know.
   - **Method** — interviews (participant, role, date), open questions, themes with too little evidence.
3. Quote verbatim; never cite a quote that isn't on the board.
